# Zag Store Code of Conduct

We want Zag Store's community to be welcoming, constructive, and safe for everyone.

## Our standards

- Treat other contributors and users with respect, including when you disagree.
- Offer actionable feedback and assume good faith.
- Keep issues and pull requests on topic.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, spam, or troll.
- Do not share others' private information or signing credentials.
- Do not use this project to promote piracy, certificate theft, or other unlawful activity.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and restrict participation when necessary.

## Reporting

If you experience unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers privately using a verified contact method listed at [zagstore.app](https://zagstore.app). Avoid including sensitive personal details in public issues.

This policy applies to project-hosted community spaces.
